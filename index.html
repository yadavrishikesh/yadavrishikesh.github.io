Rishikesh Yadav

Extreme Value Theory · Spatio-Temporal Modeling · Bayesian Computing · Data Science · Environmetrics

I am an Assistant Professor at IIT Mandi, India, since December 2024. Before joining IIT Mandi, I was a postdoctoral research fellow at HEC Montréal and McGill University, working with Prof. Aurélie Labbe and Dr. Léo Belzile (Department of Decision Sciences, HEC Montréal), Prof. Alexandra M. Schmidt (Department of Epidemiology, Biostatistics, and Occupational Health, McGill University), and Dr. Pratheepa Jeganathan (Department of Mathematics and Statistics, McMaster University).

I received my PhD in Statistics from KAUST in 2022 under the supervision of Prof. Raphaël Huser, with the thesis “Bayesian Modeling of Sub-Asymptotic Spatial Extremes”. I obtained my M.Sc. in Mathematical Statistics from IIT Kanpur in 2016, and my Bachelor’s in Mathematics and Statistics from the University of Allahabad in 2014.

Office: F5, School of Mathematical and Statistical Sciences (SMSS), North Campus, IIT Mandi 175005, Himachal Pradesh, India.
